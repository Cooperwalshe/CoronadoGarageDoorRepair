Coronado neighborhoods

Coronado Village

The older residential core. Detached garages off alleys are common, and many are original to the house — narrow single doors, aging hardware, and openers that have been retrofitted more than once.

Coronado Cays

Waterfront throughout, which means the harshest exposure on the island. Springs, cables, and rollers here corrode faster than anywhere else we work.

Coronado Shores

Mostly tower parking rather than individual doors, but the townhome and low-rise properties nearby run standard residential doors with ocean-side exposure.

Silver Strand

The stretch running south toward Imperial Beach, with salt spray coming off both the ocean and the bay.

Country Club and Glorietta Bay

Larger lots and a mix of original and rebuilt homes. Wider double doors and heavier custom wood doors turn up here regularly.

NAS North Island housing

Base and military housing across the north end of the island. Note that on-base work may need to go through housing maintenance first — worth checking before scheduling.
